These forecasts aren't directly comparable. WEF numbers come from employer surveys covering the occupations in its dataset (about 1.2 billion jobs in 2025) and count every driver of change: AI, the green transition, demographics, and the economy. McKinsey models automation only, across the whole global workforce, so its numbers are much larger. Goldman Sachs (2023) estimated that about 300 million full-time jobs worldwide are exposed to generative AI, which isn't the same as lost, so it isn't plotted.

How much work is done by people vs. technology

As adoption rises, employers expect the share of work tasks done by people alone to fall from 47% to 33% by 2030. The share done mainly by machines and algorithms rises from 22% to 34%. WEF notes this compares shares, not the total amount of work, which is also expected to grow.
